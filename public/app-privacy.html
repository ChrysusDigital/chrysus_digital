This policy explains how Chrysus Digital (CHRYSUS) handles personal information through the Chrysus Digital mobile dashboard. For privacy questions, support, or deletion requests, email contact@chrysusdigital.gr. Our website is chrysusdigital.com.

Who can use the app

The dashboard is for our business clients and their authorized users. Accounts are provided through business onboarding; there is no public account registration in the app. Your business memberships and role determine which information and actions you can access.

The app accesses the existing service database to manage appointments, callbacks and business settings. The local demo uses sample data and does not send demo records to the live service. This policy covers the mobile dashboard; the separate voice service and its call recordings are covered by the business's service arrangements and privacy information.

Information we handle

  • Account and access information: email address, password submitted for authentication, account and session identifiers, business memberships, roles and notification eligibility. Supabase authenticates your account. The native app stores a refresh token in secure device storage, rather than saving your password.
  • Business and customer records: customer names and phone numbers, appointment dates, times, types and statuses, callback details and notes, and business settings that your role permits you to view or change. Free-text fields may contain other personal information entered by authorized users, including health-related information where relevant to the business.
  • Optional notifications: an app installation identifier, push notification token and device platform, used to deliver business alerts when notification permission and business access allow it.
  • Local state: language preferences, installation identifiers and callback read/unread identifiers. Fetched business records are held in session memory, rather than a persistent offline business database.
  • Technical information: our hosting and authentication providers process connection information, such as IP addresses, to handle requests. Backend logs and diagnostics include request routes and methods, response codes, timings, error and operation codes, and business, request, job, notification and trace identifiers. We use this information to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

The mobile app does not record calls, capture microphone audio or read your device's address book. Entering a phone number into a business record does not grant access to device contacts.

Use and sharing

We use information to authenticate users, enforce business and role access, display and update authorized records, deliver notifications, respond to support and privacy requests, and maintain the service's security and reliability. We do not sell personal information or use dashboard data for advertising.

The business client determines how its customer records are used, and we process those records to provide the service on its instructions. We manage dashboard accounts, access, security and support. Business information is accessible to authorized users according to their assigned roles.

We use the following providers for the functions described below:

  • Render: hosting the dashboard API and its operational logs.
  • Supabase: account authentication and the service database.
  • Expo, Google/Firebase and Apple: delivery of optional push notifications on Android and iOS, including notification tokens and routing information.
  • Grafana Cloud: backend logs, metrics and traces collected using OpenTelemetry. Exported diagnostic fields are restricted to operational metadata, rather than request contents, customer contact fields or credentials.
  • Google Calendar, when enabled by the business: synchronization of appointment information with its connected calendar.
  • BulkGate and delivery carriers, when SMS features are enabled: recipient phone numbers and message content needed to deliver business messages.

These providers process the information needed for their service functions. Their infrastructure may operate outside your country. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

Security, retention and deletion

Production authentication and dashboard requests use HTTPS. Access is authenticated and restricted by business membership and role. The native app uses secure storage for its refresh token. No security measure guarantees absolute protection.

We keep personal information only while it is needed to provide the service, maintain its security, handle requests or meet legal obligations. Account information is retained while authorized access is needed. Business records are retained for the business's service purposes and according to its valid instructions.

When the service ends, or the business gives valid return or deletion instructions, we arrange the return or deletion of its business data. Verified requests to delete personal dashboard accounts and associated account data are handled manually by our team. Disabling access, signing out or uninstalling the app does not itself delete your account or server records.

Authentication records, notification registrations and diagnostic records follow the same need-based retention criteria. Copies in provider backups or security logs may remain after a live record is removed, until they expire under the providers' retention controls; their use is limited to recovery, security or legal requirements. Information that must be kept by law is retained for that required purpose.

Signing out clears the app's saved native session token and session data. Language preferences, installation identifiers and local read/unread state can remain on the device. You can remove app-local state through device storage controls or by removing the app, subject to your operating system's storage and backup behavior.

Your choices and requests

You can refuse or change notification permission in device settings and continue using the dashboard.

To request access, correction, export or deletion of your dashboard account and associated personal data, email contact@chrysusdigital.gr, preferably from your account email address, and identify your business and request. We may verify your identity and authority before acting. Our team handles these requests manually; account deletion removes account data rather than merely disabling access.

For customer records managed by a business, contact that business or email us so we can coordinate with the responsible business. Deleting a dashboard user account does not automatically delete shared business records belonging to that business. Any legally required retention or other lawful limitation will be explained when we handle the request.

Where applicable, you may also request restriction of processing, object to processing, request data portability, and complain to the competent data protection authority, including the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.

Policy updates

We publish changes on this page with an updated effective date. We notify affected business administrators by email about material changes to how personal information is handled.
